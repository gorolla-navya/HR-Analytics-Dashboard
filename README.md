# HR Analytics Dashboard Using Microsoft Excel

## Project Overview

I developed this HR Analytics Dashboard using Microsoft Excel to explore employee attrition and understand workforce patterns. The project involved organizing employee data, creating analysis summaries, calculating key HR metrics, and presenting the results through charts and a dashboard.

## Tools and Techniques
- Microsoft Excel
- Excel formulas and calculated fields
- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts
- KPI calculations
- Data analysis and visualization

## Key Performance Indicators
- Total Employees
- Employees Who Left
- Attrition Rate
- Average Monthly Income
- Average Employee Age

## Analysis Performed

I analyzed employee attrition across departments, age groups, salary bands, tenure groups, and job roles. I also compared employee counts across departments to understand the workforce distribution.

## Key Findings

- The dataset contains 200 employees, of whom 50 have an attrition status of Yes, giving an overall attrition rate of 25%.
- The Sales department accounts for 47 of the 50 employees who left.
- All 50 employees who left fall within the Low salary band in this dataset.
- Employees with 0–2 years of tenure account for 29 of the 50 employees who left.

These findings highlight patterns in the available data and can help identify areas for further HR investigation.

## What I Learned

Through this project, I gained practical experience in preparing employee data, using Excel formulas, summarizing information with Pivot Tables, calculating KPIs, and presenting analytical findings through charts.

## Conclusion

This project helped me understand how Excel can turn employee records into meaningful workforce insights. The dashboard provides an overview of attrition patterns and can support further investigation into employee retention and workforce planning.
